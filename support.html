ToolPocket for iPhone

ToolPocket is an iPhone app for tracking tools, where they are stored, and who borrowed them. All data stays on your phone; there is no account and nothing to sign in to.

Contact

Email leo@dbi.ltd with any question, bug report or feature request. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no sign-up and no server. Everything is stored locally on your iPhone.

Where is my data stored?

In a private database inside the app, on your device. Deleting the app deletes the data, so export a copy first if you want to keep it (Settings → Export).

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Export the asset list as CSV or PDF from Settings, then keep that file wherever you like. There is no device-to-device sync in this version.

How do the QR labels work?

Open a tool and choose the QR label option, then print or save the label and stick it on the tool. Scanning it with the in-app scanner opens that tool directly.

Why does the app ask for the camera?

Only for two things: taking a photo of a tool, and scanning a QR label. Frames used for scanning are processed on the device and never leave it.

I marked a tool as lost — how do I undo it?

Open the tool and tap Mark as found.

The app is free — is there a catch?

No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no limit on how many tools you add.

Before you email

If something looks wrong after an update, try closing the app completely and opening it again — your data is saved as you go, so nothing is lost by doing this.
