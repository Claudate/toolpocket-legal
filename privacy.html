ToolPocket

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

ToolPocket is an iPhone app for keeping track of tools, the places they are stored, and who borrowed them. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does very little: it does not collect your data, and it has no way to send it anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. ToolPocket has no account system, no sign-in, and no server of its own. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

The app stores the information you enter — tool names, quantities, notes, photos, storage places, borrowers and loan dates — in a private database inside the app's own storage on your iPhone.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for the camera only when you choose to take a photo of a tool or scan a QR label, and for the photo library only when you choose to pick an existing photo.

  • Photos you take or pick are saved inside the app's storage on your device.
  • Camera frames used for QR scanning are processed on the device and are not recorded or transmitted.
  • The app never accesses your camera or photo library in the background.

You can decline either permission and still use the rest of the app.

Exports

The app can export your asset list as a CSV or PDF file. The export is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet, where you choose where it goes (Files, Mail, AirDrop, and so on). We never receive a copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
